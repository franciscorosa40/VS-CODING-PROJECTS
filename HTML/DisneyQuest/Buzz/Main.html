Buzz Lightyear's AstroBlaster

THE STORY SO FAR

Attendees board bumper cars and attempt to navigate over foam balls, called asteroids on the floor.

By doing so, the asteroids will be sucked up into the cabin where players can then load them into a cannon

and shoot at the other cars. If hit in the correct spot, one's car may spin around uncontrollably for ten seconds.

Usually, there are two players in a car; however, it is possible for one person can pilot and shoot at the same time.
