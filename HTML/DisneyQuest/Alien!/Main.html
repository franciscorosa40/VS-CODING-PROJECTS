Invasion! An ExtraTERRORestrial Alien Encounter

THE STORY SO FAR

A 3D shooting attraction based on the former Magic Kingdom attraction, ExtraTERRORestrial Alien Encounter; with actor Jeffrey Jones reprised his role as Chairman Clench. In the attraction, attendees ride inside a rescue vehicle to save astronauts: one player drives, and the other three shoot enemy aliens.
