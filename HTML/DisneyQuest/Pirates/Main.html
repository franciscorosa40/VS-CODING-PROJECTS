Pirates of the Caribbean: Battle for Buccaneer Gold

Arcade shooter attraction with 3D projections where attendees man a pirate ship and destroy other ships,

sea monsters, and fortresses to collect gold. One player captains the ship by steering and controlling the throttle,

while up to four gunners control the cannons to destroy other ships.
